Thank you, your order is confirmed

Your Irish Roots Pack is booked in. Here's what happens next.

1. Watch for our email

We'll email you from hello@irishrootspack.com within one working day. It includes a link to a short online intake form (about 10 minutes) that tells us which route applies to you, plus how to send us your documents. If you can't see it, please check your spam or junk folder.

2. Start gathering your documents

You don't need everything yet, but it helps to start looking out the birth, marriage and death certificates for you, your parent and your Irish-born grandparent. Our documents checklist shows what's usually needed, and our certificate ordering guide explains how to get copies you're missing.

3. Hold on to your originals

Please don't post any original documents to anyone yet, including us. We'll tell you exactly what's needed and when.
