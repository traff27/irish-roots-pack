What goes in the envelope

This is the DFA's list for an adult applying through an Irish-born grandparent, as its current page describes it. Unless stated, originals must be sent. Under-18 applications and applications through a parent who was naturalised or entered on the Foreign Births Register have their own lists on the DFA page.

Your documents (the applicant)

  • Completed, signed and witnessed application form
  • Original civil birth certificate (showing parental details)
  • Original civil marriage certificate (if applicable) or other change of name document (if applicable)
  • Photocopy of your current state-issued photographic ID (passport, driving licence or national identity card), certified as a true copy of the original by your application form witness
  • 2 separate original proofs of address
  • 4 colour photographs, 2 of them witnessed. Don't attach them to the application form.

Your parent (the one descended from the Irish-born grandparent)

  • Original civil birth certificate (showing parental details)
  • Original civil marriage certificate (if applicable) or other change of name document (if applicable)
  • Photocopy of their current state-issued photographic ID, certified as a true copy by a professional from the witness list, or their original civil death certificate (if applicable)

Your grandparent born in Ireland

  • Original civil birth certificate (showing parental details)
  • Original civil marriage certificate (if applicable) or other change of name document (if applicable)
  • Photocopy of their current state-issued photographic ID, certified as a true copy by a professional from the witness list, or their original civil death certificate (if applicable)

Your witness

  • Must know you personally, must not be a relation, and must be currently practising one of the professions on the DFA's list (for example police officer, teacher or lecturer, member of the clergy, doctor, nurse, pharmacist, dentist, lawyer, notary public or commissioner for oaths, bank or credit union manager, accountant, elected public representative, vet or chartered engineer)
  • The same witness witnesses the form and 2 of the photographs, uses their official stamp on the form, and certifies the photocopy of your photo ID as a true copy of the original
  • If they don't have an official stamp, include their business card

Paying and posting

  • The DFA fee is paid online when you submit the online form (€278 adult / €153 under 18). There's no paper form to download: you print the one the online system produces.
  • Post the pack to the address on your printed application form. The DFA has no public office, so you can't hand it in.
  • Send it by recorded post so you can track it. The DFA doesn't acknowledge receipt.
  • Don't include a prepaid return envelope. The DFA returns all your documents by recorded mail.
  • Don't put a passport application in the same envelope. Passport applications are processed in a different office.

Quick eligibility check

Three or four questions, based on the DFA's published rules. It's a guide, not a decision. Only the DFA decides.

1. Were you born outside the island of Ireland (for example in England, Scotland or Wales)?
2. Were either of your parents born on the island of Ireland (including Northern Ireland)?
3. Were any of your grandparents born on the island of Ireland (including Northern Ireland)?
4. Were either of your parents already an Irish citizen when you were born, even though they were born abroad? For example, they were entered on the Foreign Births Register before your birth (the "great-grandparent" route).

Questions

What is the Foreign Births Register?

It's how people born outside Ireland become Irish citizens through an Irish-born grandparent, or through a parent who was an Irish citizen when they were born but wasn't born in Ireland. Once you're entered on the register you're an Irish citizen and can apply for an Irish passport.

My parent was born in Ireland. Do I need this?

No. If a parent born on the island of Ireland was an Irish citizen when you were born, you're already an Irish citizen and can apply for a passport directly. Please don't buy this pack.

Does Northern Ireland count?

The DFA's rules refer to the island of Ireland, so a grandparent born in Northern Ireland can qualify. If you're unsure, the eligibility check comes first, before you order any certificates.

Who orders the certificates?

You do, directly from the official issuing offices: GRO for England and Wales, NRS for Scotland, GRONI for Northern Ireland and certificates.ie for the Republic of Ireland. You pay their fees to them. Our £99 doesn't include any certificate fees, and we don't order certificates on your behalf. We give you the exact list of what to order, then check the certificates when they arrive.

What's your refund policy?

If the DFA returns your application for a reason on our pre-post checklist, we refund the £99 in full.

The refund doesn't cover DFA fees, certificate costs, eligibility decisions, or anything we weren't shown before you posted.

To claim, email us within 30 days of the date on the DFA's return letter, with a copy of the letter.

Can you speed up the DFA's processing?

No. Applications are processed in strict date order and currently take about 12 months. No one outside the DFA can change that. What we can do is reduce the risk of your application being returned for missing or inconsistent documents.

Will my original documents be returned?

The DFA says it returns all original certificates at the end of the process, by recorded mail, to the address on your application form. You don't need to include a return envelope. Send your pack by recorded post so you can track it.

Can I add my children?

Yes. Once you're on the register, children born after your registration can be registered too. Children born before you registered can't qualify through you this way. Tell us about your family and we'll map who qualifies.

What if I'm not sure I qualify?

Use the eligibility check above. If the result is "needs checking", email us before you buy and we'll tell you honestly whether the pack is right for you.
